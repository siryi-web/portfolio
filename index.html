End-to-End Automated Training Pipeline

How I connect HR events, authoring repositories, LMS runtimes, and business intelligence into a single cohesive loop.

STEP 01

Trigger & Ingest

HRIS role change or Git release tag fires a webhook payload to the automation router.

STEP 02

Package & Provision

Node.js bundler validates imsmanifest.xml, compiles SCORM 1.2 archive, and assigns cohorts via REST API.

STEP 03

Execute & Track

Learners complete responsive modules; runtime telemetry commits scores and completion states in real time.

STEP 04

Analyze & Optimize

Completion telemetry syncs to BI dashboards and triggers automated Slack/email certificates.
